How it works

From first product to first sale in under 5 minutes.

No manual. No tutorial. Just three steps and you're running.

01
Add your products

Name, price, quantity. Add a photo if you want. Scan the barcode or type it in. Each product takes about 30 seconds. When you're done, your full inventory is live and searchable.

💡 You can import a CSV if you already have a product list
02
Record sales as they happen

Tap a product, enter how many you sold, confirm. Stock drops automatically. Revenue and profit update in real time. Works the same whether you're online or not — everything syncs when you reconnect.

💡 Scan barcodes at checkout for even faster recording
03
Share your catalog on WhatsApp

Go to Settings → Share Catalog. Copy the link. Paste it anywhere — WhatsApp, Instagram, a group chat. Customers tap the link, see your full product list with photos and prices, and message you directly to order. No app download. No account needed on their end.

💡 Your catalog updates automatically when you add, sell or edit products
